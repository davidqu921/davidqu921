## Selected Research

### Conversational AI Safety & Multi-turn Red Teaming

Developing a data-driven framework for evaluating LLM safety under adaptive, multi-turn attacks. The project transforms real human–AI interaction data into reusable attack actions and trajectories, then evaluates their effectiveness across multiple open-source language models.

`LLM Safety` `Red Teaming` `Human–AI Interaction` `PyTorch` `NLP`

### Histopathology Image Analysis

Explored architectural enhancements to HoVer-Net for automated nuclear segmentation and classification, including multi-scale feature fusion and attention-based components.

**Publication:** [Exploring Architectural Enhancements for HoVer-Net: Deep Learning-based Method of Automated Nuclear Segmentation and Classification](https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202506.1228.v1)

`Computer Vision` `Medical Imaging` `Segmentation` `PyTorch`

### Medical AI Systems

Built applied AI systems for rare-disease information retrieval, whole-slide pathology analysis, rehabilitation support, and retinal disease detection. The work included RAG pipelines, vector databases, deep-learning experiments, and backend integration.

`RAG` `Milvus` `FastAPI` `Dify` `YOLO` `Multi-agent Systems`
